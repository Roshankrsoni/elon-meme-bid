# Taste

## Communication & workflow

- Delegates long tasks and keeps the agent running with terse "continue" prompts rather than new instructions or check-ins — comfortable with extended autonomous execution. Confidence: 0.5

## Product & UX

- Wants the page usable immediately: a loading indicator should be scoped to the asset still loading (e.g. only the 3D model) rather than a full-page overlay that blocks the rest of the UI. Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers hero/3D models to auto-rotate by default (idle turntable) rather than sitting static. Confidence: 0.6
- Prefers realistic, natural rendering on human subjects and wants decorative neon/glow (e.g. cyan rim light) removed from the character itself, even when the surrounding scene stays stylised. Confidence: 0.55
